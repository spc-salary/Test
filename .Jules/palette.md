## 2024-07-30 - Accessible Neumorphic Interactive States
**Learning:** Neumorphic designs often suffer from low-contrast interactive states. Adding clear focus indicators like `:focus-visible` with high-contrast outline borders dramatically improves keyboard navigability and UX without compromising the clean, minimalist aesthetic.
**Action:** Always pair soft neumorphic box-shadow active/focus states with distinct `:focus-visible` outline rings to ensure WCAG accessibility conformance.
